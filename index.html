📚 Frequently Asked Questions
🔹 How do I deposit daily savings?
Deposit money via the deposit button on Home, then contact admin on 0770371720. A receipt will be sent to you via WhatsApp confirming your deposit.
🔹 How do I request a loan?
Go to Account → Request Loan, fill the form, and submit. Your turn is based on the loan rotation order.
🔹 When do I get my weekly payout?
Payouts happen every week based on the rotation order set by admin. Check the home page for the current receiver — their name is always in blue color.
🔹 What if I miss a daily deposit?
A deficit will be recorded as debt at midnight, and penalty interest will be applied.
🔹 How do I check my loan balance?
Go to Account → Your debt/loan balance is shown under "Loan Balance".
🔹 Can I prepay future days?
Yes. Admin can use "Cover Missed Days" or "Upcoming Days Savings" to prepay for you.
💡 Need more help?
Contact the group admin directly via WhatsApp or call 0770371720. They will assist you promptly.
